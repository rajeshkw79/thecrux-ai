# Proposal: Customer Success Automation Initiative

**Author:** Arjun Mehta | **Status:** DRAFT — Do not circulate
**Last updated:** Feb 20, 2025

## Problem

Our Customer Success team (4 CSMs) is handling 120+ accounts each. Industry benchmark is 50-70 accounts per CSM. The result:
- Response time to customer queries has increased from 4 hours to 18 hours
- Onboarding new customers takes 3 weeks instead of our promised 5 business days
- Two CSMs have flagged burnout in their last 1:1s
- Churn rate has spiked from 3.8% to 5.1% in just two months

## Proposed Solution

Implement a three-phase automation program:

### Phase 1: Automated Onboarding (March-April)
- Self-serve setup wizard replacing manual handholding
- Video walkthrough library (Meera's research shows 60% of SMB customers prefer this)
- Automated health check emails at Day 1, 7, 14, 30
- Estimated cost: INR 12-15 lakhs
- Expected impact: Reduce onboarding time from 3 weeks to 5 days

### Phase 2: Smart Alerts & Playbooks (May-June)
- Usage-based health scoring for all accounts
- Automated alerts when accounts show churn risk signals
- Standardized playbooks for common scenarios (feature adoption, renewal prep, escalation)
- Estimated cost: INR 8-10 lakhs
- Expected impact: Catch at-risk accounts 3 weeks earlier

### Phase 3: AI-Assisted Support (July-August)
- AI chatbot for tier-1 support queries (handles ~40% of current ticket volume)
- Smart routing for complex tickets
- Knowledge base auto-generation from resolved tickets
- Estimated cost: TBD — need vendor evaluation
- Expected impact: Reduce support tickets by 35-40%
