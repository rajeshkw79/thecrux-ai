# The Case Study: Shark Tank India — The Pitch That Changes Everything

## The Setup

It's Season 4 of Shark Tank India. Five sharks sit in their chairs under studio lights. The camera crew is ready. The audience of 20 lakh viewers will watch this episode next Friday night.

You're backstage. In 10 minutes, you'll walk through those doors with your startup pitch. You have exactly 10 minutes to convince at least one shark to invest.

The stakes? Your company's next 18 months of runway. Maybe its survival.

But here's what most founders don't realize: **even a no-deal is a win — if you play it right.** Twenty lakh viewers means twenty lakh potential customers seeing your brand for the first time. The question isn't just "will a shark invest?" It's "will India remember your name tomorrow morning?"

## Three Startups, Three Stories

You'll work with one of these pitches (or bring your own):

### 1. FarmLoop — Farm-to-Fork Supply Chain
**Founders:** Anand (ex-Reliance Retail, grew up on a farm) + Deepa (ex-Flipkart, IIT-M)
**Stage:** Pre-Series A | **Ask:** INR 3 crore for 15% | **Valuation:** INR 20 crore
**The hook:** "Every tomato that rots is a farmer's daughter who doesn't go to school."
**The tension:** Only 4 months runway. Are they desperate or determined?

### 2. MediBridge — AI Diagnostics for Bharat's Hospitals
**Founders:** Dr. Sneha (AIIMS radiologist) + Arjun (ex-Google AI) + Pooja (ex-Apollo ops)
**Stage:** Series A | **Ask:** INR 8 crore for 16% | **Valuation:** INR 50 crore
**The hook:** "A 7-year-old boy almost died because there was no radiologist at 2 AM."
**The tension:** INR 50 crore valuation on INR 1.8 crore ARR. Can they defend the number?

### 3. StyleBox — AI-Curated Fashion for Working Women
**Founder:** Meera Kapoor (ex-Myntra, solo founder, 29)
**Stage:** Growth | **Ask:** INR 5 crore for 20% | **Valuation:** INR 25 crore
**The hook:** "My mother deserved to feel confident in what she wears. Millions of women do."
**The tension:** Solo founder. No technical co-founder. Can she convince them she can scale?

## Your Mission

1. **Pick a pitch** (or use your own startup/business idea)
2. **Get coached** — Spin up coaching agents in parallel to prep your pitch across all dimensions
3. **Face the sharks** — Run a live simulation with 5 shark agents grilling you simultaneously
4. **Land the deal** — Or if not, maximize the brand impact of your appearance

The best outcome? All 5 sharks fight over you. The worst? You walk away with 20 lakh viewers knowing your name.

**There is no losing. Only levels of winning.**
