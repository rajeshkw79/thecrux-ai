## Product / Service

FarmLoop connects small-hold farmers (1-5 acres) directly to restaurants, cloud kitchens, and institutional buyers — cutting out 3 layers of middlemen.

**Platform components:**
1. **Farmer App** (Tamil & English) — Farmers list available produce, see real-time demand, get fair pricing
2. **Buyer Dashboard** — Restaurants and cloud kitchens browse, order, and track deliveries
3. **Cold Chain Tracking** — IoT sensors on transport vehicles monitor temperature, humidity, and transit time
4. **Demand Prediction Engine** — ML model predicts what buyers will need 3-7 days out, helping farmers plan harvests
5. **Dynamic Pricing** — Algorithm sets prices based on supply, demand, quality grade, and distance
6. **Logistics Coordination** — Route optimization for pickup and delivery across rural-urban corridors

**How it works:**
- Farmer harvests → logs on app → FarmLoop matches with buyer demand → pickup within 4 hours → cold chain transport → delivered to buyer within 12 hours → farmer paid within 48 hours (via UPI)

## Market Landscape & Competition

**Market size:**
- India's total farm-to-fork market: ~$500B annually
- Organized/semi-organized addressable market: ~$50B
- FarmLoop's initial addressable market (Tamil Nadu, fresh produce, SMB buyers): ~INR 5,000 crore

**Key competitors:**

| Competitor | What They Do | Why FarmLoop Is Different |
|------------|-------------|--------------------------|
| **Ninjacart** (Series C, $150M raised) | Farm-to-retail supply chain | Urban/metro focused. Doesn't serve rural farmers directly. No cold chain IoT. |
| **DeHaat** (Series D, $160M raised) | Farm input marketplace | Input-focused (seeds, fertilizers), not output/harvest. Different value chain. |
| **WayCool** (Series C, $120M raised) | Full-stack food supply chain | Burning heavy cash. Recently laid off 30% of staff. Asset-heavy model. |
| **Local mandis/middlemen** | Traditional intermediaries | 3 layers of margin extraction. 35% food wastage. Delayed payments (30-60 days). |

**FarmLoop's moat:**
1. **Farmer trust** — Anand is from the farming community. He speaks Tamil. He visits farms. This trust takes years to build and can't be replicated by a Bangalore startup.
2. **Hyper-local density** — 450 farmers in 3 districts means route density for logistics. Each new farmer reduces per-unit delivery cost.
3. **Cold chain IoT** — Real-time temperature monitoring reduces wastage to 8%. No competitor in this segment has this.
4. **48-hour farmer payments** — Middlemen pay in 30-60 days. FarmLoop pays in 48 hours. This alone drives farmer loyalty.

## The Story (Founder's Why)

Anand's pitch, in his own words:

> "I grew up in Thanjavur. My grandfather farmed 5 acres of paddy and vegetables. Beautiful land. Good water. Hard-working family. But every season, the middleman would show up, look at the harvest, and name a price. Take it or leave it.
>
> By the time I was 12, my grandfather had sold 2 of his 5 acres. Not because the harvest was bad — because the middleman took 60% of the value. My grandmother stopped sending my aunt to college. 'We can't afford it,' she said.
>
> I spent 15 years in corporate supply chains — Reliance Retail, building systems that move goods efficiently. And every year, I'd go home to Thanjavur and see the same story: farmers working 14-hour days, middlemen getting rich, tomatoes rotting on trucks because there's no cold chain.
>
> 40% of India's fresh produce is wasted before it reaches a plate. That's not a statistic. That's my grandfather's livelihood. That's millions of families.
>
> FarmLoop fixes the pipe between the farm and the fork. IoT sensors. Direct buyer connections. Fair prices. Payment in 48 hours, not 60 days. We've already improved farmer incomes by 30-40%.
>
> Every tomato that rots is a farmer's daughter who doesn't go to school. I'm here to make sure fewer tomatoes rot."

## Strengths (What Sharks Will Like)

1. **Genuine founder-market fit** — Anand IS the target market. He grew up in it. He spent 15 years preparing for this.
2. **Real traction** — 450 farmers, 85 buyers, 22% MoM growth. Not vanity metrics — actual transactions.
3. **Massive problem** — 40% food wastage is a national crisis. INR 5,000 crore addressable market in Tamil Nadu alone.
4. **Strong unit economics trajectory** — Already contribution-margin positive per order. Path to 5% net margin visible.
5. **Technology with purpose** — Cold chain IoT isn't tech for tech's sake. It directly reduces wastage from 35% to 8%.
6. **Farmer trust** — The hardest thing to build and the hardest thing to replicate.
7. **Clean cap table** — Simple structure, fair splits, ESOP pool in place.

## Weaknesses (What Sharks Will Probe)

1. **Only 4 months runway** — Are they desperate? Will they take any deal? Or is this confident timing?
2. **Single-state concentration** — All traction is in Tamil Nadu. Will the model work in Maharashtra? UP? Punjab?
3. **Thin engineering team** — Just Deepa. If she gets sick or leaves, the platform has no technical backup.
4. **Low net margin** — 1.9% net margin per order is thin. Needs 2x scale for logistics economics to improve.
5. **Scalability questions** — Farmer onboarding is high-touch (field visits, training). How does that scale?
6. **Cold chain asset ownership** — Do they own the vehicles? Lease? Partner? What's the capex risk?
7. **Competitive threat** — Ninjacart has $150M. What happens when they decide to enter rural Tamil Nadu?
