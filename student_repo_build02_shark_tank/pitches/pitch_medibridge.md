## Product / Service

AI-powered diagnostic platform that plugs into existing X-ray and CT scan machines — no new hardware required.

**How it works:**
1. Technician takes X-ray/CT at hospital (using existing machine)
2. Image auto-uploads to MediBridge cloud via hospital WiFi
3. AI model analyzes the image in ~90 seconds
4. Preliminary report generated: findings, severity flag (normal/abnormal/critical), annotated image
5. Critical findings trigger immediate alert to on-call doctor's phone
6. Hospital dashboard shows diagnostic throughput, accuracy, and pending reviews

**What the AI detects (current):**
- Chest X-ray: TB, pneumonia, pneumothorax, cardiomegaly, pleural effusion, fractures
- Musculoskeletal X-ray: Fractures, dislocations, degenerative changes
- CT brain: Hemorrhage, midline shift, mass effect (beta)

**Key differentiators:**
- Works with ANY X-ray machine (even 15-year-old ones). No hardware investment needed.
- Hindi and English interface. Training videos in Hindi.
- 94.2% accuracy validated against senior radiologist reads (1.2 lakh scans).
- Zero misses on critical findings across entire dataset.
- DISHA-compliance ready (ahead of upcoming Indian data protection regulations for healthcare).

## Weaknesses (What Sharks Will Probe)

1. **Valuation is aggressive** — 28x revenue multiple for a INR 1.8 crore ARR company. Even for healthtech, this is a stretch.
2. **Healthcare sales cycles** — 3-6 months to close a hospital. 120 in pipeline but conversion rate matters.
3. **DISHA regulatory risk** — Upcoming regulations could change requirements. What if MediBridge's compliance approach doesn't align with final rules?
4. **Hindi-belt concentration** — 45 hospitals in Bihar/Jharkhand/UP. Will the model work in South India? West India? Different languages, different healthcare ecosystems.
5. **Dependency on 3 co-founders** — If Sneha leaves, the medical credibility walks out. If Arjun leaves, the AI walks out. If Pooja leaves, the sales pipeline dries up. Each co-founder is a single point of failure.
6. **Blume's pro-rata** — Existing investor has pro-rata rights. Does this complicate the round for new investors?
7. **Accuracy ceiling** — 94.2% accuracy means 5.8% incorrect reads. In healthcare, even 1% error rate is scrutinized. What's the path to 99%+?
